Decision rules
The focal target is the first identifiable study in each original string. Concatenated neighbours are flagged separately. Identity asks whether all focal citations represent one study; cross-report recurrence asks whether at least one supported focal study occurs in both reports. A mixed identity can still contain genuine recurrence. Verified study versions may share identity; age uses the actually cited version. Unresolved outputs are allowed.

Every case and record must occur exactly once in the fixed JSON schema. Quotes must be verbatim from that record or supplied frozen evidence, contain at most25words, and be nonempty for resolved cited-year, outlet or source-type fields. Reasons contain at most30words. No tools or external model browsing are provided. A schema failure permits one fresh-context retry. Two invalid outputs produce explicitly coded unavailable decisions. Original invalid outputs remain archived; code abstention is not a model judgment. Connection-failure resends require the separate bounded authorization described below. HTTP, credential, provenance and accounting failures stop new requests.

Aggregation and interpretation
Two matching genuine votes produce majority; three produce unanimity. Code abstentions do not count as model votes. Availability is reported separately, and complete output requires all scheduled calls. Agreement and Fleisskappa concern the three configurations; they are not accuracy estimates. Provisional ledger references and DOI-equality pairs support conditional reference agreement, not independently established sensitivity or specificity. Distinct DOI strings and false merges are not automatically false-sharing gold controls. No RoganGladen correction is generated from unvalidated reference labels.
